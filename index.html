03 / Engineering approach

Build to understand. Test to believe.

01

First principles

I like projects where abstractions can be peeled back: kernels, runtimes, storage engines, protocols, compilers, and learning systems.

02

Evidence over claims

Reproducible builds, automated tests, CI, mutation testing, numerical checks, and failure analysis matter more than long feature lists.

03

Document the edges

A useful engineering artifact should explain what works, how it was validated, and where its limits still are.
